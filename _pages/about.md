My name is Zhangqi Duan, and I also go by Gavin. I'm a third year PhD student at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, working with [Prof. Andrew Lan](https://people.umass.edu/~andrewlan/) in the Machine Learning for Education (ML4Ed) lab. My primary research focus on LLM post-training and alignment. My research spans profile-based user simulation, controllable code generation, and interpretable LLM systems. Welcome to check my recent works in Publications! 

Previously, I received my MS in Computer Science and BS in both Computer Science and Math at UMass Amherst as well. During the master program, I have worked at the UMass Amherst Center for Data Science as a machine learning engineer intern to conduct researches on the #StopAsianHate movement using NLP techniques. At the meantime, I have worked as a Teaching Assistant for CS 240: Reasoning Under Uncertainty since 2022.
